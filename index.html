About Us

Created for the World.

Rooted in Jodhpur, Rajasthan — a city renowned for its rich heritage of craftsmanship — Aditya Handicrafts is an established manufacturer and exporter of premium furniture and lifestyle products, serving discerning international buyers across global markets.

With a diverse portfolio spanning indoor, outdoor, storage, kitchen and accent furniture, alongside distinctive bone inlay collections, barware, pet accessories and houseware, we offer a comprehensive sourcing destination for retailers, hospitality groups, designers and global sourcing partners. Our wholly owned manufacturing infrastructure brings together skilled craftsmanship, disciplined production and rigorous quality control.

"Craft that outlives its era."
Vision

To be recognized globally as a trusted source for Indian handcrafted furniture and décor — bringing together traditional craftsmanship with the consistency, quality and reliability of modern international markets.

Mission

To translate India's artisanal heritage into thoughtfully crafted products for the world's leading retail and hospitality markets, through disciplined quality control and responsible manufacturing.
